The grammars are embedded in the client binary and read only from memory: there is no
download, extraction, token cache or write to the installation directory.

The tokenizer that runs them (internal/editor/textmate) is a Go port of vscode-textmate's
algorithm, MIT: internal/editor/textmate/LICENSE.vscode-textmate.md.
Its Oniguruma-compatible regular expressions come from a modified copy of
github.com/dlclark/regexp2 v1.12.0, MIT: internal/editor/textmate/internal/regexp2/LICENSE;
the changes are listed in internal/editor/textmate/internal/regexp2/NOTICE.md.
