JetBrains Mono 2.304 — https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono/tree/v2.304
The JetBrainsMono-*.ttf files are unmodified static fonts from the 2.304 distribution.
Copyright and redistribution terms: OFL.txt and AUTHORS.txt.

These committed resources are copied into Contents/Resources/Fonts by package-app.sh
and preview.sh during builds. CoreText registers them at process scope by reading the
bundle; runtime code never modifies these files. They remain part of the signed app
and the immutable baseline used for Sparkle differential updates.
